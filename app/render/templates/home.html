Why not a deepfake detector?

Detectors lose about half their accuracy on real-world fakes. Ours called every genuine voice fake. A score is not proof, so Pramaan treats detectors as hints and checks what a fake cannot change.

Who acts on the result?

You get one clear next step. The bank's analysts review each campaign once. Pramaan never blocks a payment and never calls anyone a fraudster.

What do we keep?

Only what you send, deleted after 24 hours unless you report it. Private numbers are masked before any AI sees the text. No contacts, no location, no face recognition.
