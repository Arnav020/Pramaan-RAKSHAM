The last second before the money moves

Most scams end at the payment screen. A bank app can call Pramaan's Lookup API with the payee's UPI ID, in under a second, before the customer confirms.

  1. Type a payee. Try profitwealth@ybl after an analyst has confirmed its campaign on the Desk, or a look-alike such as sbi-kyc-update.in.
  2. If Pramaan knows something, the app pauses calmly: what we know, a 30-second pause, and "call someone you trust".
  3. "Pay anyway" stays possible. Pramaan never blocks a payment; the bank decides its own friction.
  4. A payee with no warning passes silently, so honest payments stay fast.

API: GET /api/v1/lookup?q=<UPI ID> with the bank's X-Pramaan-Key. See For banks.
