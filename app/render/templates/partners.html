Pramaan for banks

Pramaan is run by a bank's Fraud Risk Management unit, under the CISO, for its own customers. The bank owns the channel, the analysts and every decision; Pramaan supplies the checks, the proof and the evidence trail. It never blocks a payment and never contacts outside bodies by itself.

02 Calling the API

GET /api/v1/lookup?q=profitwealth@ybl X-Pramaan-Key: <partner key> {"result": "warning", "headline_en": "Matches a scam campaign confirmed by bank analysts", "campaigns": [{"id": "C-…", "title": "Stock-market 'VIP group' investment scam · profitwealth@ybl", "confirmed_on": "…"}], "seen_30d": 4, "facts": [ … ], "advice_en": "Do not pay. …"}

Every endpoint is described in the generated API reference. The public, without a partner key, is limited to 20 checks per 10 minutes, so nobody can test scams against Pramaan at scale.

03 Rollout

P0

One bank, forward-to-check

The bank's WhatsApp number, about 10,000 customers, the Desk staffed by its FRM team.

P1

Inside the bank app

Share button, and the pay-time lookup on new payees (non-@valid, tagged "investment").

P2

A shared campaign feed

Confirmed campaigns shared across banks, I4C and SEBI, so one bank's review protects everyone's customers.

P3

Sealed by default

Banks seal every notice and SMS (Pramaan Seal now, C2PA for media); unsealed "official" messages become suspicious by themselves.

06 A 12-week pilot with one bank

QuestionAnswer
Who runs itThe bank's Fraud Risk Management unit, under the CISO: about a quarter of one analyst's time for the Desk and appeals. The communications team seals the bank's own notices.
What the bank providesA sandbox AWS account in Mumbai and a security review; its official domains, numbers, SMS headers and @valid handles; read access to its mule-account list; DoT FRI access; 10,000 opted-in customers.
What it costsEstimate: about ₹14,000–17,000 a month in cloud cost for 10,000 users at 2 checks each (under ₹2 per user per month). Not a quote.
Weeks 1–4Security review and deployment, then a staff-only trial with real suspicious messages staff receive.
Weeks 5–1210,000 customers; weekly rule review; go / no-go on agreed targets: 0 genuine bank messages red, ≥ 95% of reds are scams, appeals answered within a working day.

The full plan, including success criteria and what happens after the pilot, is in the repository: docs/PILOT.md.
